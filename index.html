Bulk import

Upload or paste CSV/TSV data — three formats are recognised automatically:

  • Company list: columns name (required), sector, water.
  • Full ledger export: columns Company (required), Sector, Category, Name, Title, Source, Accessed, Access status — this is exactly what the Export tab's "Download .csv" produces, so you can edit it elsewhere (or export from another session) and feed it straight back in.
  • Company directory: a target-list research sheet with columns like Company Name (required), Trading Name, Sector, Primary SIC Code, Region, Headquarters Location, Turnover (£m), Employees (UK), Companies House Number, Website URL, Source(s). Tab-separated (.tsv) files are handled automatically as well as comma-separated.

Column order doesn't matter and unrecognised columns are ignored. Companies already in the queue are matched by name rather than duplicated; leads and not-accessible entries already recorded are skipped rather than repeated. Rows with more columns than the header (usually an unescaped comma or tab inside a name) are flagged rather than imported, so they can't silently shift data into the wrong fields.

Usage reminders

  • Record only information that is published without login, paywall, or bypassing access controls.
  • Run LinkedIn lookups from your own logged-in account, in line with LinkedIn's terms of use. This tool does not query LinkedIn on your behalf.
  • Suggested lawful basis under UK GDPR: Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). You must confirm that this basis fits your specific use case, disclose it on first contact, honour opt-outs, and define your own retention period.
